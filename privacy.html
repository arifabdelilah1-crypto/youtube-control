Privacy Policy

Short Viral · Last updated: 13 November 2026

Short Viral is a private publishing dashboard. It lets its owner and team schedule and publish their own short videos to their own social media accounts (YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok). It is not offered to the public.

What we access

  • TikTok: your basic profile (open ID, display name, avatar) and permission to upload and post the videos you choose to your own account.
  • YouTube, Facebook and Instagram: the permissions you grant to upload, schedule and publish your own videos and read their basic statistics.

How we use it

Only to publish the videos you select, at the time you choose, and to show their status in the dashboard. We do not read your messages, followers' data or anything unrelated to publishing.

Storage and security

Access tokens are stored encrypted on our server (Supabase Vault) and are never shown in the browser. Videos are stored on our own server only as long as needed to publish them.

Sharing

We never sell or share your data with third parties or advertisers. Data is only sent to the platform you publish to.

Your choices

You can disconnect at any time from the dashboard or in your TikTok, Google or Meta account settings (apps and websites). Ask us to delete your data and we will remove your tokens and stored videos within 30 days.

Contact

Questions or deletion requests: contact the Short Viral team through the developer contact listed on our app page.
